This is a weak field and you are already the best-equipped firm in it.

Neither competitor we checked has analytics, a tag manager, an enquiry form or LocalBusiness markup. You have all four.

The problem is not that they are ahead of you. It is that your own site is nearly empty.

The Row That Should Change How You Think About This
Next Generation Roofing has no way to contact them from a mobile

780 words, eleven pages, and no tappable phone number and no enquiry form we could find. A homeowner on a phone reading their site has to memorise a number and type it manually, or leave.

Bratt Builders has been trading since 1958 and publishes 123 words. One page. Sixty-eight years of reputation and a website you could read in thirty seconds.

So the bar in Birmingham roofing is on the floor. You are not fighting sophisticated marketing — you are competing against firms who have not really tried.

Which means the opportunity is not to out-spend anyone. It is simply to be the firm that looks professional and can be contacted. You are already most of the way there.

Where You Are Ahead, And Where It Stops
Ahead
  • The only analytics in the field. You can see what works; they cannot.
  • The only tag manager — which means adding a Meta pixel or conversion tracking is minutes of work for you and a project for them.
  • The only LocalBusiness markup, which is what tells Google you are a real business in a real place.
  • The only enquiry form, and three tappable numbers against their one and none.
Where it stops

Next Generation Roofing publishes eleven pages to your six. Thin pages — but a buyer searching for one specific service is more likely to find a page about it on their site than on yours.

And your two weakest pages are the two that matter most at the point of decision: a 41-word contact page and a 25-word gallery. Their sites are thin all over; yours is thin exactly where it costs.

What We Could Not Check
Marked honestly rather than guessed
  • Inner pages on both competitors. We read homepages and sitemaps, not their full sites.
  • Everyone's Google Business Profile, reviews and map position, including yours. On local roofing a great deal of enquiry begins there.
  • Whether anyone here advertises, and what it returns. The Meta Ad Library has refused our requests all week, so no advertising by any firm is described or assessed anywhere in this document.
  • Anyone's revenue, crew size, pricing or win rate, including yours.
  • Every roofer in Birmingham. This is the set a search returned and we could verify — a city that size has hundreds, and the larger commercial contractors will not appear in a domestic search at all.

A firm trading since 1958 with 123 words, and a firm with eleven pages you cannot ring.

You do not need to beat them. You need to finish your own site.
