The Short Version
  • Your contact page is 41 words and your gallery is 25. Those are the two pages someone reads when they have already decided they might use you. Your privacy policy is the second longest page on the site.
  • Your whole site is 2,567 words across six pages, with one services page covering everything.
  • One client some weeks, ten others — that is not a lead volume problem. More advertising raises the peaks; it does not raise the troughs. What raises troughs is work that recurs — landlords, agencies, maintenance, commercial buildings that need something every year. Your site does not mention any of it.
  • Roofing is bought on photographs. Nobody can assess a roof from the ground. Your team finishes work every week and the gallery has 25 words on it.
  • You are the best-equipped roofer we checked in Birmingham. The only one with analytics, the only one with a tag manager, the only one with LocalBusiness markup, the only one with an enquiry form. Next Generation Roofing publishes eleven pages and has no tappable phone number at all. Bratt Builders has traded since 1958 and publishes 123 words.
  • The bar in this market is on the floor. You do not need to out-spend anyone — you need to finish your own site.
  • Your sitemap declares one page of six, and there is no Meta pixel — though the tag manager is already installed to add one in minutes.
